# CRM staff operating guide

Prepared 21 October 2026. Training and review dates are not booked yet.

Start at Sales pipeline → Lead Desk. Use one lead record through the whole conversation; update it instead of entering the same inquiry again. If the CRM cannot save, keep a temporary record in the approved team channel, tell IT and enter it once service returns. Do not treat a failed save as completed work.

## Three actions staff need most

1. **Capture:** Quick add → Lead. Choose the brand, source, buyer/supplier type and salesperson. Add the company and what is known. Save; complete the details as the conversation develops.
2. **Work:** Assigned to me or New for me → Update / feedback. Acknowledge receipt, record what actually happened and select the next follow-up date. Acknowledging is not a customer response.
3. **Finish the day:** Check Follow up today and Needs feedback. Save the latest outcome and next date for each open lead. If it is not relevant, explain why; do not quietly abandon or delete it.

Only record contact that occurred. Saving Contacted does not send an email or WhatsApp message. In the connected CRM it records an audited update and can notify the lead creator internally; browser-local preview does not deliver those notifications.

## Choose the right lead type

| Type | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| Buyer inquiry | An actual request for a product, price or quotation. |
| Buyer prospect | A possible customer, researched company or imported contact without a confirmed buying request. |
| Supplier prospect | A possible sourcing partner not yet offering a confirmed supply opportunity. |
| Supplier offer | An actual offer from a supplier. Track sourcing updates; do not request a customer quotation from this record. |

Website, email, WhatsApp, social and advertising inquiries belong in the same Lead Desk. Until IT confirms a channel is connected, enter its inquiries manually and select the real source. Check for an existing record before re-entering the same inquiry. Keep a new inquiry separate when the buying requirement is genuinely different.

## Handoff from Marketing to Sales

Marketing or an authorised allocator chooses one active Sales account. If the salesperson is not listed, use Tools → Manage team only if authorised, or ask IT/MD to confirm their account and brand access. People & HR entries alone are not assignment accounts.

Use Needs assignment to find open leads with missing owners or known non-sales owners. Older records with no verified owner-role metadata still need a manual review; the CRM does not guess their role.

The salesperson checks New for me, acknowledges and records the next real outcome. Marketing can still see leads they created after assignment. If an owner changes, the new owner must give fresh feedback; earlier feedback remains history. Review the queue even if a notification is missed.

## Qualify once, reuse the details

Before qualification, record the product, grade, quantity, unit such as MT or KG, destination/port, customer company, contact person, email or phone and responsible salesperson. Ask for missing details; do not fill them with guesses.

For B2B trading, confirm whether the person is a buyer or supplier, the relevant decision-maker and company identity. Add agreed destination, timing and commercial requirements when known. Verify sensitive payment/bank or contracting details independently under the company's approved process.

| Business stage | How it appears in the current CRM |
| --- | --- |
| Inquiry | New / Contacted. Record source and actual conversation outcome. |
| Qualification | Qualified, after the required commercial fields are complete. |
| Quotation | Create a linked quotation; obtain the existing approval and mark it sent only when actually sent. Quote Sent on a lead alone is not evidence of an issued quotation. |
| Negotiation | Negotiation, with an updated outcome and next date. |
| Order | Accepted quotation creates the linked order and operational/accounting handoffs under existing controls. Confirm the actual order; do not duplicate it. |
| Closed | Record the outcome. Reports count conversions only when backed by linked confirmed orders, not Won alone. Not Relevant requires a reason. |

Do not bypass quotation approval, financial locks or a read-only role. Supplier offers stay separate from customer demand; customer proformas are not supplier invoices or proof of payment.

No quota or response-time threshold is imposed by this guide. MD must approve it first. Missing CRM entries are a process exception to investigate, not automatic proof of poor effort.

## Weekly review

IT, Liera or the MD may run the review. Select the facilitator for each session; being a facilitator does not widen CRM permissions.

1. Select the brands and creation dates being reviewed. In Tools, export the weekly report after a successful refresh. Clear source/type/owner/search filters for a consolidated review.
2. Review new and qualified buyer inquiries, sent quotations, confirmed conversions, overdue work, missing next actions and missing feedback. Inspect brand, product/grade, owner and source summaries.
3. Discuss the few exceptions that need action: no owner, no feedback, overdue follow-up, missing qualification data, sourcing gaps or delayed deliveries. Record one owner and one deadline for each decision.
4. Check any response-time figures against their sample count. Unknown timestamps stay unknown. These are elapsed, staff-reported contact timings, not business-hour SLA measurements.
5. Review the previous actions and agree next week's priorities. Save the dated export and action log in the approved company location.

The report follows leads created in the selected period, with their currently linked quotations/orders and current state at the snapshot. It is not a historical end-of-week snapshot or a separate count of all quotations sent that week. It cannot measure staff who have no visible records or conversations never entered in CRM.

Review action log columns: Review date | Facilitator | Brand | Lead/reference | Exception | Decision | Action owner | Due date | Completion evidence. Record decisions, not credentials or speculative performance scores.

## Staff training plan

Participants: Liera, Aleena, Mak and Max. Suggested trainer: IT with MD process approval. Confirm the trainer, dates and individual brand coverage before booking sessions.

| Proposed session | Duration | Exercise and pass condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1: capture | 30 minutes | Enter one fictional inquiry and one prospect with the correct brand, source and type; find the saved records. |
| Day 2: ownership and feedback | 20 minutes | Sales receives an assignment, acknowledges, records a real-world test outcome and next date. Liera/IT reviews permitted feedback; Not Relevant has a reason. |
| Day 3: qualify and quote | 30 minutes | Aleena and Max complete required fields and demonstrate the quotation approval path. Liera practises read-only review. |
| Day 4: handoff and reporting | 20 minutes | Mak finds the permitted order/shipment handoff and records progress. IT/Liera/MD explains one grouped report and its date/response definitions. |
| Day 5: supervised practice | 20 minutes | Each participant completes their role's daily routine without help; capture questions and correct the process. |

Use fictional records for exercises. Do not create real quotations, invoices, payments or messages just to test training.

## Digital account control

IT maintains a restricted company register: service/channel, brand, company owner, named administrator, MFA enabled, recovery owner, last access review and offboarding action. Verify these in the actual domain, hosting, mailbox and advertising services. Use an approved password manager for secrets; never store passwords, API tokens, recovery codes or private keys in lead notes, exports or this guide.
